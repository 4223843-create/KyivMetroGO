EXACT GEOMETRY & CURRENT BUG MECHANICS:
1. `.sheet-handle-bar` is the fixed header. It has a diagonal hatched background pattern (`background-image: url(...)`) and a height of 52px (Y-range: 0px to 52px).
2. `.sheet-station-title` ("Хрещатик") is absolutely positioned inside `.sheet-handle-bar` at `top: 40px`. With font size and line height, the rendered text spans from Y = 40px to Y = ~62px.
3. `#sheetBody` starts immediately below the header at Y = 52px. It has a solid background (`background-color: var(--bg-sheet)`) and `padding-top: 18px`.
4. As a result, the first actual content text inside `#sheetBody` ("попередня Театральна") sits at Y = 70px (52px + 18px padding).
5. THE BUG: Because `#sheetBody`'s solid background starts at Y = 52px, it renders OVER the lower half of `.sheet-station-title` (from Y = 52px to 62px), slicing the station name "Хрещатик" horizontally in half! Furthermore, when the user scrolls, scrolling content protrudes over the title text.

- DO NOT change `top: 40px` or font size/line-height of `.sheet-station-title`.

- DO NOT alter the visual spacing/margins between the station title and the content below.
